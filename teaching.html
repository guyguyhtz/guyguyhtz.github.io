Physics

From 2010 to 2026 I taught physics at The Open University of Israel, and served as a reviewer, academic development assistant, teaching coordinator and tutor in courses such as Physics Preparatory Workshop, Fundamentals of Physics, Introduction to Modern Physics, Waves and Optics, Advanced Topics in Wave Theory, Physical Methods in Medicine, Introduction to Geophysics, Analytical Electromagnetism, and Nuclear Physics.

Before that I worked as a physics teacher at the Israel Arts and Science Academy.
